About

Hello! I'm Aris, a Computer Science & Engineering undergrad at the University of Ioannina and a full-stack developer at IQsoft in Greece.

Day to day I work on IQManager — a Next.js + Django ERP that runs the field-services side of the company — and on Tank 3 Dashboard, a real-time monitoring system for an LNG storage tank with hundreds of sensors. On the side I build smaller tools for 3D printing and a Greek-market e-shop.

Outside of code I run, cycle and play football. I'm happiest when a messy real-world problem turns into something a non-technical user can pick up without a manual.
