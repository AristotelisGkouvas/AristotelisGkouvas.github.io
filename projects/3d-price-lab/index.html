3D Price Lab.

Drop an STL, OBJ or 3MF and get a defensible quote — material, electricity, labour, machine wear, margin and VAT, in any currency. Files stay in the browser.

Overview

Most 3D-printing quoters either undercharge (because they only count filament) or overcharge (because they pad a flat margin). I wanted a tool that prices a print the way an engineer would — by adding up the actual costs and being transparent about each one.

The whole pricing engine runs in the browser. STL/OBJ/3MF files are parsed locally with custom WASM-friendly code, the model is previewed with Three.js, and the receipt updates live as you tweak the cost model. Nothing ever leaves your machine.

Why it matters

Anyone running a small printing shop can self-host this and get a quote calculator that respects their margins, currency and local VAT rules — without subscribing to yet another SaaS.

01.

Highlights

Drag & drop import

STL, OBJ and 3MF parsed entirely in the browser, with a Three.js previewer for orientation & sanity checks.

Engineering cost model

Material price, energy at slot tariff, labour rate, machine wear-per-hour, margin and VAT — every line itemised.

Multi-currency, multi-language

Configurable currency formatting plus i18n via i18next. Switch the UI between Greek and English on the fly.

Privacy-first

No file upload, no analytics, no third-party calls. Self-hostable as a single static bundle.

Tested & type-safe

Strict TypeScript across the codebase, Vitest unit tests on the pricing engine, ESLint + lint-staged hooks.

Themeable UI

Three built-in themes (Blueprint, Studio, Light) and tokens that make new themes a one-file change.
