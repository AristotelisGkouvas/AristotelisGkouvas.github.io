3DPrinto eShop.

A WooCommerce storefront for a 3D printing studio with a custom plugin for STL upload and instant quoting, plus a full Greek-market stack: myDATA invoicing, multi-courier shipping and Viva Wallet payments.

Με λίγα λόγια

Ηλεκτρονικό κατάστημα για studio τρισδιάστατης εκτύπωσης. Ο πελάτης ανεβάζει το αρχείο του και παίρνει τιμή αμέσως.

Συνδεδεμένο με myDATA, ACS, Box Now και Viva Wallet — δηλαδή δουλεύει όπως το χρειάζεται στην πράξη μια ελληνική επιχείρηση.

Overview

The studio sells stock products and custom prints. Stock works fine on plain WooCommerce, but custom prints needed something new: a customer should be able to upload an STL, see an instant quote, and add it to their cart — all without staff intervention. So I built a custom WP plugin that does exactly that, hooked into Woo's product types.

On top of the storefront I integrated the rest of the Greek e-commerce stack: myDATA for invoice transmission to the tax authority, ACS / Box Now / ELTA for shipping with automatic waybill generation, and Viva Wallet for cards & instalments.

01.

Highlights

STL upload + auto-quote

Drop an STL on the product page, get an instant price based on volume, material & settings, then add to cart.

myDATA invoicing

Automatic transmission to IAPR — invoices, credit notes, retail receipts, with retry & audit trail.

Multi-courier shipping

ACS, Box Now and ELTA wired into checkout, with auto-generated waybills and live tracking links in emails.

Viva Wallet payments

Cards, installments, IRIS and pay-later flows — all routed through Viva with branded checkout.

Branded PDF invoices

Locale-aware invoice PDFs auto-attached to order emails, mirroring the myDATA submission.

Reproducible Docker stack

WordPress + MariaDB + phpMyAdmin behind Caddy, all in docker compose with a one-command bootstrap.
