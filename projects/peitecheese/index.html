ΠείτεCheese.

The best photos of a wedding are scattered across two hundred phones and never reach the couple. ΠείτεCheese collects them behind a single QR code — guests scan, upload and browse straight from the browser, with no app and no account.

Visit live site

Overview

ΠείτεCheese is a multi-tenant platform for collecting the media of an event. One deployment hosts many independent events; each gets an unguessable URL, its own gallery, its own albums and its own storage namespace. The organiser prints a QR code, puts it on the tables, and every guest becomes a photographer.

The whole design turns on one number: how many people bother. So the guest path has zero friction — no install, no sign-up, no permissions dialog. Open the link, see the gallery, upload, leave a wish in the guestbook. Everything that needs an account lives on the organiser's side.

Buy once, publish once

There is no subscription. A Stripe payment mints one Activation — a reusable credit that publishes a single draft event at a given tier. Building an event is free; spending a credit is the publish gate. Tiers cap photos and videos separately and enforce per type on upload, so a customer never hits a limit they were not sold.

Private by default

The S3 bucket has no public policy. Every image reaches the browser through a short-lived presigned URL, thumbnails and display-size derivatives are generated lazily and cached, and the originals are kept untouched for the final ZIP export.

Highlights

One QR, no app

Guests scan a printable QR and land straight in the gallery. No install, no account, no permission prompts — the single biggest barrier removed.

Live wall

A projector-ready slideshow that cycles new uploads as they arrive, plays videos inline and keeps a scan-me QR in the corner. It also demos the product to every guest in the room.

Activation credit model

One-off Stripe payments mint reusable credits; applying one publishes a draft event at its tier. No subscriptions, no recurring billing, no dunning.

Presigned, private storage

Media lives at events/{id}/… in a private bucket, served only through expiring presigned URLs. Uploads are validated by type and size, login endpoints are rate-limited.

Everything back in one ZIP

The organiser downloads the whole event as Album / Uploader / file in original quality, plus the guestbook as a printable page, plain text or CSV.

Albums, moderation, analytics

Owners and co-hosts sort media into albums, remove anything they should not keep, and watch uploads, uploaders and engagement per event on a Recharts dashboard.
